What we do

We specialize in SAP: ABAP and SAP Fiori development for SAP ERP and S/4HANA. We design complete custom solutions and find a way even with unusual problems.

  • ABAP development

    Reports and transactions, enhancements to the standard, object-oriented development, HANA data models, BOPF and BRF+.

  • SAP Fiori

    SAPUI5 and Fiori Elements apps, hybrid apps and Fiori Launchpad setup.

  • Integration and interfaces

    OData, REST and SOAP services, RFC, IDocs and proxies; data exchange in XML, JSON, EDI or Excel, for example with warehouse systems.

  • Public registers and government systems

    Connecting SAP to Czech public registers and government services, such as electronic sales registration (EET) or records management under the national standard.

  • Print forms

    Print output and forms in Adobe PDF Forms, from layout to data binding.

  • Complex custom applications

    Large tailor-made applications including approval workflows and custom document storage, built on SAP Fiori.
